## Question
At a fixed window budget, which compaction policy leaves an agent most able to act on a planted constraint and a planted identifier, and does behaviour follow what the policy mechanically keeps?

## What is already known without a model
The deterministic survival grid (`results/survival/grid.json`, tagged `deterministic`: real policy code on seeded histories, no model) shows what each non-model policy keeps. With the rule planted at turn 1 (the default) and a budget of 2400 tokens, `recency_truncate` kept it in 0 of 20 histories, `head_tail` in 20 of 20 and `observation_mask` in 20 of 20; at a budget of 1800, `observation_mask` kept it in 0 of 20 because its fallback to dropping history fired (ADR-019). This experiment asks whether agent behaviour follows those survival rates. It does not test survival itself.

## Hypotheses (directional, stated before running)
- **H1** `recency_truncate` violates more than `head_tail`. Why: the first drops the rule and the second keeps it verbatim (grid above). ConstraintRot reports the same ordering on its harness (docs/PRIOR_ART.md).
- **H2** `llm_summary` (prompt v1) violates more than `head_tail`. Why: a generic summary may drop or reword the rule; `head_tail` cannot reword it.
- **H3** `observation_mask` at budget 2400 violates no more than `control` plus noise. Why: it keeps every user turn, so the rule survives verbatim (grid above). This is a prediction that could fail, for example if masked tool outputs confuse the model.
- Where survival is verbatim, I expect violation near the `control` rate; where it is gone, near the `floor` (chance) rate. The interesting results are the exceptions.

## Design
- Packs: `constraint` and `identifier` (ADR-020). Policies: `recency_truncate` (target 0.6), `head_tail` (head ratio 0.3, target 0.6), `observation_mask` (keep 2, target 0.6), `llm_summary` (prompt v1, keep 2 units). Window limit 2400 tokens. Conditions: `floor`, `control`, and per policy `treatment` and its `length_matched` twin.
- Models: at least one free model that passes `ctxcheck smoke`; ids entered here before freezing: `________`.
- Seeds: design target 27 per cell; achieved n stated in the report (ADR-018). Per model and pack, the plan is at most 11 calls per seed (2 shared for floor and control, 4 policies x 2 for treatment and length-matched twin, 1 for the summary). `ctxcheck run --dry-run` prints the estimate per policy. Free-tier daily limits decide how many days that takes; `ctxcheck run --dry-run` prints the estimate.
- Temperature: provider default, recorded. Trials: 1 per seed. Only synthetic seeded scenarios are sent.

## Primary comparisons (three, per model and pack, chosen before looking)
1. `treatment(recency_truncate)` vs `treatment(head_tail)`. 2. `treatment(llm_summary)` vs `treatment(head_tail)`. 3. `treatment(observation_mask)` vs `control`. Paired by seed. Benjamini-Hochberg across the three. All other comparisons are exploratory and labelled.

## Analysis plan
As EXP-001: Wilson intervals per cell, exact McNemar, paired bootstrap risk difference, `not_applicable` shown and covered by the sensitivity analysis. Command: `ctxcheck report results/EXP-002`. A policy that ends in its fallback (`observation_mask`) or in `irreducible_budget` is reported with its count, not hidden in the rate.

## Validity checks
Same as EXP-001: `floor` violation below about 50%, `control` violation above about 20%, or more than 20% `not_applicable` makes that model-pack result uninterpretable. In addition: if verbatim survival in the trace disagrees with the deterministic grid for the same policy and seed, the harness has a bug and nothing is reported until it is found.

## Interpretation rules (written in advance)
- A hypothesis is supported for a model if the paired risk difference is in the predicted direction, its 95% bootstrap interval excludes zero and the Benjamini-Hochberg adjusted exact McNemar q is below 0.05 (both, because the bootstrap is optimistic at small n; see EXP-001). Directional with an interval that includes zero is reported as inconclusive. An opposite-direction difference is reported as such.
- Agreement between survival and behaviour is described per policy from the exploratory table; no pooled "survival predicts behaviour" claim is made from a handful of policies.

## What would change our mind / what would make us stop
Change: a validity check fails, or the manual audit finds the summaries rewording the rule in ways the verbatim check misses (then a labelled judge is considered as a secondary measure only). Stop: free-tier caps. No optional stopping.

## Manual audit
Read at least 20 `llm_summary` outputs; record how the rule and the identifier were transformed.
