## Question
As the window budget shrinks (so compaction must remove more), how do survival of the planted fact and the violation rate change, for `llm_summary` and for one deterministic policy?

## What is already known without a model
For the deterministic policies the survival side of the curve needs no model and is already in `results/survival/grid.json` (tagged `deterministic`). It has a step shape, not a gradual slope. With the rule planted at turn 0, `head_tail` keeps it at every budget tried (20 of 20 at 1200 tokens), but planted at turn 1 it loses it at 1200 (0 of 20) and keeps it from 1800 up: even "keep the head" has a budget threshold. `recency_truncate` loses a rule planted at turn 6 or earlier at every budget tried, and the budget at which it starts to keep a rule depends on how late the rule was planted (turn 9: 3600; turn 12: 2400; turn 15: 1800; turn 17: 1200). A smooth dose-response is therefore not guaranteed for the behavioural side either, and the experiment is built to show a step if there is one.

## Hypotheses (directional)
- **H1** For `llm_summary`, violation does not fall as the budget shrinks (a tighter budget gives the summarizer less room to keep detail). Expected direction: violation non-increasing in budget.
- **H2** For `recency_truncate`, violation changes from the floor level to the control level at the budget where the rule first survives (taken from the grid), not gradually.
- Honest expectation: for `llm_summary` the budget changes what is summarised (more or less history) but not the size of the summary, which is capped by `max_summary_tokens`, so a flat curve is plausible. A flat curve is a result.

## Design
- Pack `constraint`. Budgets (window limit in tokens): 1200, 1800, 2400, 3000, 3600 (the pre-probe window is about 3.8k, so each forces compaction). Policies: `llm_summary` (prompt v1) and `recency_truncate`. Conditions per budget: `floor`, `control`, `treatment`, `length_matched`.
- Floor and control do not depend on the budget; their cassettes are shared across budgets, so they are paid for once.
- Models: at least one free model that passes `ctxcheck smoke`; ids entered before freezing: `________`.
- Seeds: design target 27 per cell; achieved n stated (ADR-018). One budget at 10 seeds is at most 50 calls for `llm_summary`; five budgets are about five times that for the treatment rows, minus the shared floor and control calls. The report says how many budgets were completed.
- Temperature: provider default, recorded. Synthetic scenarios only.

## Primary comparisons (two)
1. `llm_summary` violation at the tightest budget (1200) vs the loosest (3600), paired by seed. 2. `recency_truncate` violation at the largest budget below which it loses the rule vs the smallest above it (chosen from the grid before running). Benjamini-Hochberg across the two. The full curve is exploratory and shown with Wilson intervals at every budget.

## Analysis plan
Per budget: Wilson interval per cell and verbatim survival (`ctxcheck report` groups cells by window limit). The figure is violation and survival against budget with intervals; no line is fitted through points that the data do not support, and no smooth curve is drawn between budgets.

## Validity checks
As EXP-001 and EXP-002, per budget. A budget at which `control` fails is excluded and listed.

## Interpretation rules (written in advance)
Report the shape as observed: monotone, step, flat, or noisy. "Dose-response" is claimed only if violation changes in the predicted direction across budgets with the paired interval excluding zero for the end-point comparison. Otherwise the report says no dose-response was detected at the achieved n.

## What would change our mind / what would make us stop
Change: a validity failure. Stop: free-tier caps; the budgets completed are reported as completed, not extrapolated.

## Manual audit
Read at least 10 summaries from the tightest and 10 from the loosest budget and note the difference in how the rule is carried.
