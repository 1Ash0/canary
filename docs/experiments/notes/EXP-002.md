### What this shows, and what it does not
- With the rule planted near the start, **what a policy keeps decides what the agent does**: the policies that keep the opening turns (head and tail, masking) leave the agent as good as full context; the one that drops them leaves it guessing. That is what the deterministic grid predicted, now confirmed with recorded behaviour on two canaries.
- It is partly true by construction: a rule planted early is exactly what head and tail keeps. A rule planted in the middle of a session, or one stated twice, is a different test (EXP-003 varies the position).
- The summary sits between the two, and depends on the model writing it: Mistral's summaries carried the rule almost every time, gpt-oss-20b's lost it often, as in EXP-001 and EXP-001b.
- Free models, one budget, one prompt version, scripted history, a single compaction. No claim about models in general.
