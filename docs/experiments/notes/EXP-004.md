### What this shows, and what it does not
- **Which policy is safe depends on where the fact sits.** Drop oldest loses early facts, keep head and tail loses the middle and late ones, and each time the agent then fails on exactly the fact that was lost. EXP-002's "keep head and tail is as good as full context" was a property of a rule planted at turn 2.
- **Masking tool output kept every fact it had room for**, and failed only where its fallback had to drop turns. That fallback is the honest cost of the policy at a tight budget.
- **The summary did well here** with Mistral, which carried the rule through in EXP-002 too. It is one model; EXP-001 and EXP-002 show that a summary's quality depends on which model writes it.
- Each seed's summary was written once and reused by the three packs (the summary request holds the history, not the final request), so within a seed the three facts were judged against the same summary.
- One model, one budget, scripted history, one compaction, synthetic facts. No claim about models or harnesses in general.
