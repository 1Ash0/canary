# 2026-10-21 — EXP-001 at the planned 27 seeds: the effect depends on the summarizing model

*Drafted by Claude Code. Numbers live in `docs/experiments/EXP-001.md` (Results at the planned 27 seeds), generated from `results/EXP-001/summary.json` and checked by `scripts/check_claims.py`. This entry repeats few of them.*

## What was done since the pilot
- Mistral `ministral-14b-latest` extended from 10 to 27 seeds.
- Groq `openai/gpt-oss-120b` cleared and re-run from seed 1 so every summary uses the real 400-token cap; it stopped at 4 seeds on its daily token cap and the owner chose not to resume it. The 10-seed pilot is frozen in `results/EXP-001-pilot10/`.
- NVIDIA NIM: ten models tried, five passed the tool-call smoke test, five 3-seed pilots (`PILOT-002`) showed only `openai/gpt-oss-20b` writes a real summary every time. It then ran 27 seeds as an optional third model under addendum B.

## What the data say
- **Two models that summarise disagree.** NVIDIA's `gpt-oss-20b` shows the effect clearly and passes every preregistered criterion for that model. Mistral shows a null: its summaries almost always kept the channel name, so the rule survived and the agent obeyed.
- **The agent follows the summary, mostly.** For Mistral, behaviour matched what the summary kept in every episode. For NVIDIA it matched far less often: it sometimes chose correctly after losing the rule and sometimes broke it after keeping it. That is the part worth reading by hand in the audit.
- So the headline is not "compaction erases rules". It is "whether a generic summary carries the rule depends on the model writing it, and the agent mostly does what the summary allows".

## What this does not show
No claim about models in general. Groq is unfinished, so addendum A's two-model condition (Groq and Mistral) is not met and I claim no cross-model replication. NVIDIA's model is the same family as Groq's. NVIDIA's trial terms (evaluation only) are unverified for publishing. One prompt, one pack, free models that change.

## Process notes, kept on the record

3. Free-tier practicalities that shaped the work: Groq's daily token limit, Gemini's bursts of 503, Mistral's 429s, NVIDIA's list of models that no longer work. The retry loops and per-day caps are in the run commands, not in the experiment design.
