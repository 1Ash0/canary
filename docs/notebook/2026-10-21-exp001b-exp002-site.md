# 2026-10-21 — EXP-001b and EXP-002 recorded; the site becomes an instrument

*Drafted by Claude Code. Numbers live in the Results sections of `docs/experiments/EXP-001b.md` and `EXP-002.md`, generated from their `summary.json` by `scripts/write_results.py` and checked by `scripts/check_claims.py`. This entry repeats almost none of them.*

## What was done
1. **EXP-001b** (preregistered, frozen before running): the NVIDIA `gpt-oss-20b` arm of EXP-001 again, with `reasoning_effort: low` on the summary call so reasoning no longer eats the summary's token cap. No summary was cut off, and the effect replicates against control and against the length-matched control. Groq's arm waits for a new UTC day (its daily token cap).
2. **EXP-002** (frozen before running): four policies, Mistral on both canaries and gpt-oss-20b on the channel rule, 27 seeds. Every validity check passes. Dropping the oldest turns loses the early rule and the agent guesses; head-and-tail and masking keep it and the agent behaves as with full context; the model summary is in between and model-dependent.
3. **The site** (ADR-022): the Lab, ASCII window views, results as one tab per experiment with a mark per seed that opens the episode, the Lab's "what real models did with this window" line, the recorder's text view and transcript, the /about beats, a copy pass, and lazy section loading.

## What went wrong, and what it taught
- **The analysis pooled the two canaries.** Cells were grouped by model and limit, so EXP-002's packs merged (n=54) and seeds were paired across packs. Caught in a preview report before any result was written. Lesson: a new dimension in the data (pack) needs a test that it is never pooled; there is one now, on both sides.
- **The preregistered comparisons had no home in code.** `ctxcheck report` ran the default comparisons. Now `results/<exp>/analysis.json` names the primary set and the BH family; exploratory tests are adjusted separately so they cannot dilute the primary ones. Written after Mistral's data existed, so it is logged as a deviation even though it only transcribes the frozen text.
- **The ledger replay rejected every masked trace.** Placeholders keep the step of what they replace. Grading never used the replay, so no number moved, but the site export broke. Fixed with a two-sided test.
- **"Lazy" sections were not lazy.** Each section was a dynamic import, but all were imported at startup. Measured, not assumed: first-view JS was over budget. Now sections mount as they approach. It is still slightly over budget when the Lab is on screen, and that is written down rather than rounded away.
- **Commits went through on a failed check twice** (`check.ps1 | tail` hides the exit code). Every commit since is gated on the check's exit code.
- **Turn numbering differed between pages** (from 0 on the grid, from 1 in the Lab). Everything counts from 1 now.

## What it means
The policy comparison says something useful and unsurprising: with a rule planted early, whether a policy keeps the opening turns decides the outcome, and behaviour follows survival almost exactly. The interesting results are the exceptions: gpt-oss-20b twice broke a rule that was still in view after masking, and summaries are the one policy whose outcome depends on the model. The next experiment that would teach something is EXP-003 (move the rule to the middle of the history), where head-and-tail stops being safe by construction.
