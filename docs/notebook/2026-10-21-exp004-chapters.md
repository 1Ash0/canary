# 2026-10-21 — EXP-004 (three facts), the site in chapters, and an expert audit

*Drafted by Claude Code. Numbers live in `docs/experiments/EXP-004.md`, generated from `results/EXP-004/summary.json`.*

## Why EXP-004
EXP-002 planted one rule near the start, so "keep head and tail" kept it by construction and looked as safe as full context. That was a statement about position, dressed up as one about policies. EXP-004 plants three facts (turns 2, 9, 16) in one seeded history and asks for one of them per episode.

## What it found
Which policy is safe depends on where the fact sits: drop oldest loses the early rule, keep head and tail loses the middle and late facts, masking keeps whatever it has room for, and the agent fails on exactly the fact that was lost. Every preregistered prediction held or was not contradicted.

## What went wrong
- **My own preregistered table was slightly wrong.** I computed "what each policy keeps" on the account-code request's window and assumed the window was the same for all three requests. The request is part of the window; the channel request is longer, and on 5 seeds masking hit its fallback and dropped the rule. The trace showed it, the offline parity run reproduced it exactly, and it is logged as a deviation, not hidden.
- **The parity check had a bug** (it called the last tool listed); it crashed on the three-tool packs and is fixed.
- **Summaries are shared across the three packs** (identical summary requests share a cassette). That is fine by design, and now stated.

## The site
The long landing is now four chapters (Lab, Compare, Results, Method), each ending with the next; a chosen episode travels in the URL. The audit fixed an overgeneralised headline, a view mixing two experiments, a statistic from the wrong experiment, a headline missing its condition, and missing limits. The chart no longer says "named in the summary" for policies that make no summary.
