How Canary measures forgetting

1 · Plant
A fact goes into the run early: a rule, an exact value, or a failed approach. That fact is the canary.
2 · Compact
The window fills and the harness compacts it with the policy under test: truncating, summarising or masking.
3 · Probe
The agent is asked to do something where the fact matters, and answers with one tool call.
4 · Grade
The emitted tool call is parsed and graded by deterministic code, with no model as judge in any headline number. Behaviour is what counts: a summary can mention a rule and the agent can still break it.
Survival of the text
A secondary check. A fact is kept if its exact words are still in the window and gone otherwise. A paraphrase counts as gone, so this undercounts survival.
Uncertainty
Every rate is shown with a 95% Wilson interval, because a rate without an interval is an unverified claim. Same-seed comparisons are paired, so only trials where two conditions disagree count toward the difference.

Prior art, credited first

The design we replicate
  1. arXiv 2606.22528 Chen. Governance Decay: How Context Compaction Silently Erases Safety Constraints in Long-Horizon LLM Agents. Its abstract reports violation rising from 0% to 30% after a single compaction (up to 59% on the worst models), measured on seven models. These are the paper's numbers, not ours, and we have not yet reproduced them.

What is new here
Nothing yet. The contribution is practical: something a builder can point at their own compaction policy and watch, with a recorder, a test kit and honest intervals. A null result will be reported as one.
